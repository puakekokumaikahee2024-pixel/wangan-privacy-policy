## Information We Collect

- **Purchase history:** processed via Apple's in-app purchase system (Unity IAP)
- **Advertising Identifier (IDFA):** used by Google AdMob to serve reward ads
- **Player name & scores:** entered voluntarily for online rankings (stored on Supabase)
- **Crash logs:** collected automatically for debugging purposes

## How We Use Information

- Purchase history: to restore purchases and validate entitlements
- IDFA: to display rewarded video advertisements via Google AdMob
- Player name & scores: to display online leaderboards within the app
- Crash logs: to identify and fix technical issues

## Third-Party Services

- **Google AdMob** — advertising (see Google's privacy policy)
- **Supabase** — leaderboard data storage
- **Apple App Store** — in-app purchases

## Data Retention

Online ranking data is retained until the player requests deletion. Contact sandhiego2000@yahoo.co.jp to request removal.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.
